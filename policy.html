2-Day Return & Exchange Policy
We want you to love your Urban Rich garments! If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you can request a return or size exchange within 2 days of delivery.

• Doorstep pick-up will be arranged for size exchanges.
• Refunds are processed back to your original payment method or store credit within 48 hours of quality check.
