About Urban Rich

Dress the Urban. Own the Rich.

Our Story & Vision

Founded in the heart of urban street fashion, Urban Rich was born out of a desire to create clothing that balances streetwear culture with effortless elegance. We believe that what you wear is an extension of your identity.

Premium Craftsmanship

Every garment is constructed from 100% bio-washed super combed cotton, heavy-duty denim, and custom-dyed fabrics engineered to retain shape, color, and texture wash after wash.

Modern Silhouettes

From oversized drop-shoulder tees to barrel-fit trousers and baggy raw denim, our fits are meticulously tailored for maximum comfort and confidence.

Why Choose Urban Rich?

Customers across India trust Urban Rich for our hassle-free 2-day returns, express shipping, and unmatched streetwear quality.
